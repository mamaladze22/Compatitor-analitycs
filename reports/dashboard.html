Subscription Race
Recurring revenue is the competitive frontier
Palitra L has been building a subscription funnel for 2+ years. Biblusi just entered this week with Litbox. Sulakauri has no subscription product. This gap is growing.
Dominant Mechanic
1+1 bundle is the market's standard promotion language
Both Biblusi and Palitra L run 1+1 promotions. It is the default Georgian book market promotional hook — familiar and expected by the audience.
Untapped Mechanic
Scarcity and urgency are deeply underused
Intelekti's 5-hour flash sale is the only pure urgency play in the entire scan. No other competitor is using time-gating. There is white space here for Sulakauri.
Format Gap
Video is almost absent — one competitor owns it
Palitra L's subscription explainer video (57 days) is the only durable video creative in the set. Everyone else runs static. First-mover advantage for any competitor that invests in video.
Language Signal
Georgian-only copy confirmed across all 6 competitors
Not a single English-primary ad in the full set. The Georgian-speaking parent or reader is the universal target. English-language copy has zero role in this market's paid acquisition.
Platform Anomaly
Palitra L favors Facebook + Messenger over Instagram
Most of their ads run Facebook/Messenger only, skipping Instagram. Unusual placement choice in 2026 — may signal an older demographic target, or deliberate cost efficiency.
